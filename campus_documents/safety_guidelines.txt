1. Emergency SOS
Students can use the SOS button on the Student Dashboard to report emergencies. Press the SOS button when immediate assistance is needed. Security personnel should respond promptly to SOS alerts.

2. Incident Reporting
Students can report falls, medical emergencies, suspicious activities, and other safety incidents through CampusGuard AI. If a student notices suspicious activity, they should report it through the Incident Reporting feature and provide relevant details. Students should avoid confronting or approaching a suspicious person.

3. Restricted Areas
Students should not enter restricted areas without authorization. Security personnel should investigate unauthorized entry.

4. After-Hours Safety
Students should follow campus timings. Security personnel should monitor unusual activity after hours.

5. Emergency Response
Security personnel should acknowledge alerts and take appropriate action. Critical incidents should be escalated immediately.
